### Added

- In a save's system view, drag a megastructure to move it within its system. A Hyper Relay,
  a Gateway or the Shroud Seal goes to any point.
  - A Science Nexus and other structures that stand beside their planet drag like a moon. They
    stay where you drop them and keep their planet. Right-click one and select Snap to put it
    back beside its planet.
  - An Orbital Ring, an Arc Furnace or a Dyson Sphere sits on its body. Drop it on another
    planet, moon or star to move it there. The bodies that can take it are ringed while you
    drag.
