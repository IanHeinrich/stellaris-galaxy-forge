### Added

- Delete a megastructure from a save. Right-click it in the system view, or use the
  button on its page.
  - Deleting a Hyper Relay or a gateway also removes its link to the others. The planet
    under a megastructure stays, and a new one can be built there.
  - A colony on the planet stays. On a planet the megastructure made, such as a
    Cosmogenesis World, the colony is removed and the planet stays.
