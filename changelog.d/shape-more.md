### Added

- The Shape tool draws spirals. A spiral has 1 to 6 arms of systems winding out from its centre.
- An outline can be up to 5 systems thick.
- A circle, polygon, rectangle or star outline can have smaller rings inside it.
- Gaps break an outline's lanes in evenly spaced places, which makes chokepoints.
- Wobble moves each system of an outline or a grid a little at random, so the shape looks less exact.
- A filled circle, polygon or star can leave its centre empty, like a donut.
- A circle, polygon or spiral outline can have a system at its centre. Spokes can join that system to the outline.
