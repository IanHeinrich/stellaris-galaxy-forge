### Changed

- The Special menu of Add system lists a mod's layouts that only an event or
  another system places, such as most of Gigastructures' systems. They are
  grouped under the name of the mod that adds them. Random systems never use
  them.

### Fixed

- A mod's system layout or scripted variable that replaces the game's now
  wins, as it does in the game. Planetary Diversity's Sol neighbours and
  Gigastructures' Dyson sphere system were losing to the game's versions.
