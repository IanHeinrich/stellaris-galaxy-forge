### Changed

- Gateways, Hyper Relays and L-Gates you add now go where you click.
- The pictures in the Add megastructure dialog show much sooner the first time.
- Messages about megastructures are clearer, such as why one can't be added
  or moved.

### Fixed

- Large resource amounts under a planet or megastructure in the system view no
  longer run into each other.
- Dragging a megastructure no longer stutters. It turns to its new facing when
  you drop it.
