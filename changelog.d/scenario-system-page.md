### Added

- A scenario system's page and its planets' pages show what the
  initializer places, in the same sections as a save.
  - Values the game rolls show as ranges.
  - Planets the game may not place have a "may not spawn" mark on the
    pages and a dashed ring in the system view.

### Changed

- The line under a system's name counts planets only. Moons and
  asteroids are counted in the Planets header.
