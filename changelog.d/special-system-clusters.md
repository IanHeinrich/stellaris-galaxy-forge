### Added

- The Junk Ratlings and the Legendary Leader's sites can now be added from
  the Clusters group of the Special list, each as one edit. Their systems
  are placed around the spot you right-clicked, joined to each other and
  to the systems nearby. Shuffle places them again.
- The Junk Ratlings land within the distance the game uses. The Legendary
  Leader's sites land 10 to 30 from its crash site, joined to it.
- In a save, a cluster comes with the flags and event targets its story
  looks for. If the save already holds them, the cluster's story stays
  with the systems that have them.
- The Junk Ratlings' home and the Legendary Leader's crash site can also be
  added on their own, without the rest of their cluster.

### Changed

- In a scenario, "New special system…" opens the Special list, clusters
  included, in place of the initializer list. The Legendary Leader's crash
  site makes its own sites there, so it is listed on its own.
