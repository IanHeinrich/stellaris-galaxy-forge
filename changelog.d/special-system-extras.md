### Added

- Special systems you add come with their own anomalies and dig sites. Their
  card in the Special list shows what each comes with and what it
  lacks, such as its empire, its fleets or some of its planets.

### Changed

- The game's events about a special system you add now find it, so its
  story can start. If the galaxy already has that system, the story stays
  with the one already there, and the card says so.
