### Changed

- The Add megastructure list leaves out placeholders, arkships and other
  structures that are really ships, crisis and event structures such as
  the Galactic Crucible, and ruins that have no repair.
- With Gigastructural Engineering, a Dyson Sphere, Dyson Swarm,
  Matrioshka Brain or Star Lifter is one row. Adding it writes the
  version the game builds at that star.
- Giga's wrecked warships are offered as wrecks.
- Each tile shows its place in the row, such as "3 of 4". The details
  panel shows the game's description with its colours and icons, what
  the megastructure produces and gives its owner, and what adding it also
  writes. The Add button names the stage it adds.
