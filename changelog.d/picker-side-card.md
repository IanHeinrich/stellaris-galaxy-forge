### Changed

- In the deposit, modifier, anomaly and dig site pickers, each row shows its first two effects. A card beside the picker lists every effect and the description of the row you point at.
- A planet's modifiers show their first two effects and how long they last. Point at one to see every effect in the same card.
- The blocker picker lists the clearing technologies in one "Cleared by" drop-down instead of a chip each, so the list keeps its room in a narrow inspector.
- Inspector fields line up on every page and fill the width of the inspector. Long choices end with an ellipsis instead of running past the edge.
