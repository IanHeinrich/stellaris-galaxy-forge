### Added

- Open a script file in your own editor at the line you're looking at.
  Galaxy Forge finds VS Code, VSCodium, Notepad++, Sublime Text, JetBrains
  IDEs and Kate, and asks which one to use the first time.
  - Change the editor or forget your choice in the Help menu.
- A scenario system's Source tab shows its initializer under the system's
  own text. Click an `inline_script` line's name to open that script.
- Scenario planets and moons have a Source tab. It shows the initializer
  with the body's block marked.
