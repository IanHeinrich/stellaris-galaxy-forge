### Added

- A scenario planet's page shows what the game can roll for it: its
  class, deposits, planet features and ring. Odds show as bars where the
  game's files give them.
- Unnamed planets and moons in a scenario show the name the game gives
  them, such as "Yunak II".
- Planets the game never places show "never placed" and look faint in
  the system view.
- A scenario system's inner radius and belt radii show as ranges where
  the game rolls them.
- A small dial beside a planet's angle step shows where it can land
  relative to the planet before it.

### Changed

- Scenario pages are shorter. Explanations have a dotted underline and
  show when you hover over them.
- Dig sites are called archaeological sites, as in the game.
