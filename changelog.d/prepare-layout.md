### Changed

- Prepare for a new game says for every choice what you get in the new
  game, whether this map or the game decides it, and whether the editor
  shows what you'll get. Point at a row to see a card with every choice.

  game, with Apply always in view. "Not now" shows the whole page.
- While Prepare for a new game is open, the map rings the systems that
  become ordinary stars, that the game rolls, and the new seats and
  zones. A legend explains the colours.
- The scenario's name has its own field at the top of the Galaxy page.
