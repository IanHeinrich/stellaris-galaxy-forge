### Changed

- Prepare for a new game uses the galaxy setup screen's words. Every
  choice shows who places those things in the new game: Galaxy Forge,
  the game, or no one. Hover over a row to see a card with every choice.
- The presets are Keep everything, New empires and Layout only.
- After Open as scenario, the Galaxy page shows only Prepare for a new
  game, with Apply always in view. "Not now" shows the whole page.
- The map rings the new starting positions and fallen empire zones Galaxy
  Forge draws, with a legend.
- The scenario's name has its own field at the top of the Galaxy page.
- The tool rail stays in the system view, with only Select.
