### Added

- Raise or lower systems above the galaxy plane in a save, the way the
  game shows them in 3D. Set one system's height on its page, or several
  at once from the Inspector.
- A Height brush (H) sets, raises, smooths or ripples heights under the
  brush. Presets make ripples, waves, a dome or a crater.
- A Tilt slider tilts the map to show the heights, with a mark at the
  game's own camera angle. You can still move systems and edit lanes
  while tilted.
- Raised and sunk stars show a coloured ring on the new Heights layer.
