### Added
- Long pickers, such as the planet class picker, have a filter box. Type part of a name to narrow the list.

### Changed
- The planet class picker shows each class's planet art.

### Fixed
- Modded planets that take their surface from another model show their art. This covers More Arcologies' arcologies and Planetary Diversity's tidally locked worlds.
- City worlds show their art again with More Arcologies enabled.
- Planet icons show the planet's own art when a mod names an icon it never defines.
