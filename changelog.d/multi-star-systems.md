### Added

- Add system here offers binary and trinary systems, under "Binary stars"
  and "Trinary stars". Each is named by its stars, and a companion star
  can have planets and moons of its own.
- Random can roll a binary or trinary system, about as often as the game
  does.
- Sol can be added with its neighbours Alpha Centauri, Procyon and Sirius,
  from the Clusters group of the Special list. Each neighbour can also be
  added on its own.
- More special systems with two or three stars can be added from the
  Special list.
