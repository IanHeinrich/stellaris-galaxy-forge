### Added

- Add system here has a "Binary or trinary" menu. Random binary and
  Random trinary roll one as the game would.
- "Build one…" builds a binary or trinary from stars you choose, any type
  in any slot, in one of the game's four arrangements. Planets are
  generated to suit each star, or left out with "No planets".
- A companion star can have planets and moons of its own.
- An added binary or trinary's page can change its arrangement, its
  stars and the icon it shows on the galaxy map.
- Random can roll a binary or trinary, about as often as the game does.
- Sol can be added with its neighbours Alpha Centauri, Procyon and
  Sirius, from the Clusters group of the Special list. Each neighbour can
  also be added on its own.
- More special systems with two or three stars can be added from the
  Special list.

### Changed

- "Join to nearby systems" in the Special list starts unticked.
