### Added

- Prepare for a new game, on a scenario's Galaxy page. Choose what a new game
  takes from the map: starting positions, starting systems, Sol, guaranteed
  habitable worlds, fallen empires, marauders, leviathans, enclaves,
  pre-FTL civilizations, unique systems, wormhole pairs and system names.
  - Every choice shows who places those things in the new game: Galaxy
    Forge, the game, or no one. Hover over a row to see a card with every
    choice.
  - Keep everything leaves the map as it is. Keep the galaxy gives every
    empire a random starting system. Keep the layout keeps only the star
    positions, hyperlanes and nebulae, and draws new starting positions
    and, on Paint a Galaxy maps, new fallen empire zones. The game rolls
    everything else.
  - Starting positions can be drawn again at random, spread out as the
    game spreads empires. On Paint a Galaxy maps, Fallen empires can get up
    to six new zones.
  - The map rings the new starting positions and zones, with a legend.
    Reroll draws them again.
  - The Sol row keeps Sol, turns it into a normal system, puts pre-FTL
    humans on Earth, leaves it to the game, or keeps it for the United
    Nations of Earth on Paint a Galaxy maps.
  - Keep leviathans, marauders and L-Gates away from starting positions is
    on by default. Systems the game would roll within 2 jumps of a starting
    position become normal systems.
  - The Wormhole pairs row warns you when removing the pairs cuts systems
    off.
  - Apply is one step to undo.
- After Open save as scenario, the Galaxy page shows only Prepare for a new
  game, with Apply always in view. "Not now" shows the whole page. It is
  also in the File menu.
- The Issues tab notes systems near a starting position that have no
  initializer. The game fills them at random, so a leviathan or a marauder
  home can land there.

### Changed

- The scenario's name has its own field at the top of the Galaxy page.
- The tool rail stays in the system view, with only Select.
