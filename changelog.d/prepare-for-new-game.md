### Added

- Prepare for a new game, on a scenario's Galaxy page. Choose what a new game
  keeps from the map: empire seats, home starts, Sol, fallen empires,
  marauders, guardians, enclaves, primitives, wormhole pairs and system
  names. Each row says what the game does with what you leave out.
  - Faithful keeps everything, and Fresh start turns what old empires and
    events made into plain systems. Bare shell keeps only the shape of the
    galaxy. It draws new capital positions at random, spaced as the game
    spaces empires, and new fallen empire zones on Paint a Galaxy maps. The
    game rolls everything else, system names included.
  - Empire seats can be drawn again at random. Each new seat takes an old
    seat's kind in turn.
  - On Paint a Galaxy maps, Fallen empires can get new zones at random, up
    to six, wherever the map has room.
  - The Sol row keeps Sol, turns it into a normal system, puts pre-FTL
    humans on Earth, leaves it to the game, or keeps it as the United
    Nations of Earth's seat on Paint a Galaxy maps.
  - Reroll draws the seats and zones again.
  - Keep the space around capitals clear is on by default. Systems the
    game would roll within 2 jumps of a capital get a plain system instead.
    Otherwise a leviathan or a marauder home can land next door.
  - The Wormhole pairs row warns you when removing the pairs cuts systems
    off.
  - Hover a row to see its systems on the map.
  - Apply is one step to undo.
- After Open save as scenario, the Galaxy page opens with Prepare for a new
  game ready to use. It is also in the File menu.
- The Issues tab notes systems near a seat that have no initializer. The
  game fills them at random, so a leviathan or a marauder home can land
  there.
