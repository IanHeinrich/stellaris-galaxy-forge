### Added

- In a save's system view, right-click a body or empty space to add a megastructure. Most
  types your install and mods define can be added. Pick a megastructure, then one of its
  stages, in a dialog that shows each stage's model and description. The megastructure goes to
  the empire that owns the system.
  - Each megastructure is listed once, with its stages, ruins and variants inside it. Types
    from mods such as Gigastructural Engineering are grouped the same way.
  - Ring worlds, L-Gates, the later Quantum Catapult stages, Deep Space Citadels and
    waystations can't be added yet. Types that change their planet's class or build a whole
    structure show greyed out with the reason.
  - The megastructure is added finished, with the modifiers, deposits and counters its
    construction adds. An Arc Furnace's mining bonus shows on its system's planets. Other
    build effects don't run, so a Dyson Sphere doesn't freeze the system's planets.
