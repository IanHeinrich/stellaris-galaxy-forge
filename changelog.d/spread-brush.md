### Added

- The Spread brush (R) evens out the spacing of the systems under it, in
  saves and scenarios. Drag it over a clump to spread it out, or over a gap
  to close it. Hold Alt to gather systems towards the brush's centre instead.
- Lanes stay attached as systems move, and a move stops short of crossing
  another lane. Special and home systems stay in place unless you tick the
  box to move them.
