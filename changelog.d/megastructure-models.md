### Added

- In a save's system view, megastructures and starbases show as their models from
  the game, at their in-game size. Click one to open its page.
  - Each one shows its name under it. A megastructure drawn over a star or planet,
    such as a Dyson Sphere, has its name under the body's name.
  - With System details on, a megastructure shows its base output each month, before
    empire modifiers.
  - Habitats and ring world sections show as their models too. The sections face
    their star, so they join into a ring.
