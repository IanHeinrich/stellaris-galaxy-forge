# Nebulae <Badge type="tip" text="Save" /> <Badge type="info" text="Scenario" />

Turn on the Nebulae [layer](../map/layers.md) to see and edit nebulae
on the map.

## Move or resize a nebula

Drag a nebula's ring to move it. Select it and drag a handle on the
ring, or press <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd>, to resize it.

## Add a nebula

Right-click empty space and select "New nebula here". The new nebula uses a
name from the game's own name list.

## Rename or delete a nebula

You can rename a nebula on its page. To delete one, right-click it and
select "Delete nebula", or click "Delete nebula" on its page.
