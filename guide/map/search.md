# Search <Badge type="tip" text="Save" /> <Badge type="info" text="Scenario" />

<kbd>F</kbd> opens search. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> and <kbd>/</kbd>
open it too.

## Find a system, empire or planet

Search finds systems, empires, planets, fleets and nebulae by name. It
also finds systems by what is in them, such as "gaia", or by their
precursor, such as "Vultaum". In a scenario it finds systems and nebulae.

![Search open with "gaia" typed, listing Gaia World systems while the map rings them](../images/map/search.png)

<kbd>Enter</kbd> jumps to the highlighted result. <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>,
or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click, adds its system to the selection and keeps
the search open. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

In a save, search also finds star types. Type a type's name, such as
`pulsar` or `black hole`, its letter, such as `g` or `class g`, or a
colour, such as `yellow` or `red`. The Star types group lists each
matching type with the number of systems that have it. Press
<kbd>Enter</kbd> on a star type to ring all its systems and fit the map
to them. The rings stay until your next search. Press
<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> to add all its systems to the
selection instead.

## Search one kind of thing

Start the search with a prefix to find only one kind:

| Prefix | Finds |
| --- | --- |
| `s:` | Systems |
| `e:` | Empires |
| `p:` | Planets |
| `f:` | Fleets |
| `n:` | Nebulae |
| `t:` | Star types |

For example, `e:vex` searches empires for "vex". <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves
what you typed to the next kind, from everything to systems, empires,
planets, fleets, nebulae, star types and back to everything.

## Pin a search

Pin a search to see the systems it finds in every file you open. They
show a coloured ring on the map. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> pins
the search, or unpins it. The
Pinned searches tab in the dock lists your pins, and so does the empty
search box.
