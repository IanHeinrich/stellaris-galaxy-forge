# Supported game versions

Galaxy Forge works with Stellaris 4.x saves and scenarios.

## What I've tested in game

- The galaxy map edits, on 4.4.6 and 4.5.
- The system view, planet, empire and wormhole edits, on 4.5 only.
- Scenarios, on the same two versions.

I test with all DLC and no mods. I haven't tested Ironman saves.

## Edit a save from before 4.0

Hyperlane editing also works on saves from 3.4 through 3.9. Adding
systems and planets, copying planets and editing anomalies need a 4.x
save.

## After a game update

Moving systems and editing nebulae touch parts of the save that rarely
change between versions. Adding systems and editing stars, planets,
colonies, empires and wormholes go deeper into the save. Those are the
edits most likely to need a fix after an update.

If the art or names from your install stop working, the map falls back
to plain stars and generated names. Anything Galaxy Forge doesn't
understand is copied through as it was.

## Use mods

Galaxy Forge reads the mods in your playset, so modded systems show with
their own names and art. See [Game data and mods](../start/game-data.md).
Load an edited save with the DLC and mods it was made with. See
[Go back to your campaign](../safety/back-to-campaign.md).
