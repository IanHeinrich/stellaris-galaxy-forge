# Fallen empires, marauders and L-Gates <Badge type="info" text="Scenario" />

A scenario can say where fallen empires and marauder clans go. The game
places leviathans and the L-Gate itself.

## Add a fallen empire zone <Badge type="warning" text="Paint a Galaxy" />

On a map for Paint a Galaxy, right-click a system and pick "Add fallen
empire zone". Drag the ring to move it, and drag from a system's ring
onto it to choose what the fallen empire connects to.

A system's page has Add zone in its Fallen empire zone section too.

![A scenario system's page, with its Spawn point, Fallen empire zone and Initializer sections](../images/scenario/scenario-system-inspector.png)

## Add a marauder clan

Right-click empty space and pick "Add marauder clan here". Or select
three systems joined by hyperlanes and pick "Make these marauder clan N"
in the Inspector. The middle one becomes the clan's home.

## Keep threats away from starting positions

[Prepare for a new game](prepare.md#keep-leviathans-marauders-and-l-gates-away-from-starting-positions)
turns the systems near each starting position into normal systems, so
no leviathan, marauder clan or L-Gate spawns next to an empire.
